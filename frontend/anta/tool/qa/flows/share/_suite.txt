# The two-guest suite for `qa pass share -d emulator-5556`: two accounts on
# two fleet guests, paired, and what one shares reaching the other — the
# device proof of the sharing foundations (docs/cloud-sharing-foundations.md
# F4, the multi-user roadmap's R10) and of the share surfaces
# (docs/cloud-share-surfaces-roadmap.md). The older flows set and clear a
# share through the QA seam (`qa share`); 04g–04i give, receive and stop
# through the app's own surfaces — the category editor's and the event
# editor's switches, the Categories page's ⋮, the Sharing page and its
# sheets, the bar that says what arrived — and use no seam. Everything else
# is the app's own UI too: sign-in, bind, the pairing sheet, the calendar
# and the browser on the partner's guest, the unpair dialog.
# - Two guests: the pass's own, `anta_qa_1` on emulator-5556, is the owner
#   of what is shared (account qa1, the fixture's rows); `anta_qa_2` on
#   emulator-5558 is the partner (account qa2, an empty database), driven
#   by the steps that lead with `-d emulator-5558`. Always name the pass's
#   device (`-d emulator-5556`), or a step naming it would open a second
#   connection to it.
# - It needs the cloud-define QA build on both guests (`qa build --define
#   ANTA_QA_CLOUD=true --define ANTA_QA_FIREBASE_EMULATOR=10.0.2.2 --define
#   ANTA_QA_ACCOUNT=qa1:qa1@example.com`, then `qa fleet install`) and a
#   freshly started emulator suite (`tool\firebase\emulators.cmd`): the
#   pass leaves both accounts' spaces in it.
# - The partner's guest signs in as its own account through `qa set
#   account=…`, held in memory: the prelude sets it after that guest's
#   fresh relaunch, and nothing relaunches it until the last flow's end.
# - The flows run in order on one seed, each starting where the last one
#   left both guests — on Sync & sharing — so the suite is never sharded
#   and a red flow reddens the ones after it.
# - The partner reads what is shared with it when its engine runs, and
#   nothing tells that engine of the owner's change (it cannot watch
#   another account's space): every check on the partner's guest follows a
#   "Sync now" there. The cookbook:
#   .claude/skills/qa-emulator/references/share-pass.md.
