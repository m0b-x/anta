# The hide bar (D30). In "Categories from …" the partner hides Recovery
# while no alert of its own is on it: no bar. It then adds an alert to
# "Foam rolling" from the read sheet (armed), and hides Recovery again:
# the bar says "Recovery is hidden. Your alerts on it are off until you
# show it again." and nothing is armed; shown again, the alert is armed
# again. The owner makes Recovery private to end: the partner hears that
# Foam rolling left with its alert, nothing armed, its filter back to
# "All" — what 04i expects.
