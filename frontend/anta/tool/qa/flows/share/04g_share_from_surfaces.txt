# The owner gives through the app itself, no seam (share surfaces §6.2,
# §6.3, §6.5, §6.6, §6.14): a new category, Stretching, shared from its
# editor's switch as it is made; Gym from its row's ⋮ on the Categories
# page; the seeded "Lifting session" from its editor's switch. The Sharing
# page names them; the tick sheet takes Gym back and Done says so; the
# Single events sheet stops the event with its ✕ and Undo brings it back.
# The partner, after its sync, is told what arrived, its View opens the
# Sharing page, and its calendar draws the owner's events as the owner's.
# - Stretching is made on the device, so its id is minted: its rows are
#   named by title (`_labels.txt`), and its events by "Hip openers", an
#   event made in it today, all day (the editor's defaults).
# - "Lifting session" recurs on Mondays and Thursdays, so it is reached
#   through the Upcoming list: filtered to recurring events its Cardio card
#   stands alone at the top (unfiltered, a third card's "Show every day"
#   lies under the Add event button); a row of the card's day list selects
#   its day, and the day panel's row carries the event's seeded id. The
#   filter is put back after.
# - The Single events sheet's ✕ and the tick sheet's Cancel are the only
#   closes without an id there: the sheet is closed with Back.
# - A count is glued to its noun with a no-break space (U+00A0): the
#   "1 event" targets below carry one.
# - Runs after 04f (the pair holds, nothing is shared, both guests rest on
#   Sync & sharing, one back from the root); 04h and 04i build on what it
#   leaves shared: Stretching and "Lifting session".
