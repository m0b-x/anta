# The partner chooses what it receives and how (share surfaces §6.4, §6.5,
# §6.7, §6.8): Stretching switched off in "Categories from …" leaves its
# calendar at once, with no sync, and comes back when switched on; under
# "Category alerts too" its own sheet takes an alert of the partner's own,
# and the Categories page lists it under "From …" with "Alerts on"; the
# look row changes how a partner's row is drawn; the calendar's filter
# lists the category in a group of its own.
# - Stretching's id is minted on the owner's device: its rows are named by
#   title. In "Categories from …" the title matches the row (`--nth 0`,
#   which opens the category's sheet) and its show switch ("Show
#   Stretching on my calendar", `--nth 1`).
# - The sheets here close with Back: their ✕ carries no id.
# - "Alerts on" is glued with a no-break space (U+00A0), as a count is to
#   its noun: the Categories page target below carries one.
# - Ends with the alerts setting back on "Only alerts I add" and the look
#   on Outlined, the defaults: 04i's notice counts the alerts armed on
#   what leaves, and the category alert added here rings at 9:00 on the
#   day, armed only before then.
-d emulator-5558 scroll-to id:sharing-paired --in id:sharing-list
-d emulator-5558 tap id:sharing-paired
-d emulator-5558 wait id:sharing-page
-d emulator-5558 tap id:sharing-theirs-categories
-d emulator-5558 wait id:shared-categories-sheet
-d emulator-5558 expect "Stretching" "From qa1@example.com"
-d emulator-5558 tap "Stretching" --nth 1

-d emulator-5558 wait id:shared-categories-sheet --gone

-d emulator-5558 shot hidden-gone
