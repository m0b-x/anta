# Developer options through the unlock: five taps on the drawer header's
# icon (drawer-header-icon) close the drawer and draw the page's hub row,
# which opens the page; system back lands on the hub, and once more on the
# folder root (D2, D3).
# - The five taps must land within two seconds of each other
#   (_AppDrawerState._tapTimeout); each `tap` is one dump and one tap, well
#   inside it. The fifth closes the drawer, then shows the unlock snackbar
#   after 100 ms; the flow waits for the folder root rather than reading
#   the snackbar's words.
# - The basic.json reseed before the flow starts the app locked; already
#   unlocked, the taps do nothing and the drawer stays open.
# - The hub row is the App section's last and may sit below the fold, so it
#   is scrolled to before the tap, inside the hub's list (settings-hub-list):
#   the first scrollable in the dump may be the search field.
# - Locking is left to the widget suites: the page's Lock button carries no
#   id.
tap id:nav-menu
wait id:drawer-calendar
tap id:drawer-header-icon
tap id:drawer-header-icon
tap id:drawer-header-icon
tap id:drawer-header-icon
tap id:drawer-header-icon
wait id:create-folder
expect --absent id:drawer-calendar
tap id:nav-menu
wait id:drawer-calendar
scroll-to id:drawer-settings --in id:drawer-list
tap id:drawer-settings
wait id:settings-bar-hub
scroll-to id:settings-hub-row-developerOptions --in id:settings-hub-list
tap id:settings-hub-row-developerOptions
wait id:settings-bar-developer
key back
wait id:settings-bar-hub
expect --absent id:settings-bar-developer
key back
wait id:create-folder
expect --absent id:settings-bar-hub --absent id:drawer-settings
errors
