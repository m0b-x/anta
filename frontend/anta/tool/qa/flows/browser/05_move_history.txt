# A move, then its history: Session 1 moved out of Week 1 into Training
# through its sheet's Move and the picker's Up and confirm; the ⋮ menu's
# Move history lists it, its undo puts it back, and Clear empties the
# sheet through the confirm.
# - The picker opens inside the note's own folder, where the confirm is
#   off (the same folder); Up goes to Training.
# - Each note's preview names the other, so "Session 1" matches both rows
#   of Week 1: its own row is the last match. A row is told by a word only
#   its own preview holds (Squat, Deadlift).
# - The undo's id counts entries newest first: the one move is 0.
wait "Training"
tap "Training"
wait id:create-note
wait "Week 1"
tap "Week 1"
expect "Squat" "Deadlift" --timeout 10
longpress "Session 1" --nth=-1
wait id:row-action-move
tap id:row-action-move --settle 1000
wait id:folder-picker-search
expect "SUBFOLDERS OF WEEK 1" --timeout 5
tap id:folder-picker-up
expect "SUBFOLDERS OF TRAINING" --timeout 5
tap id:folder-picker-confirm
wait id:folder-picker-confirm --gone
expect "Deadlift" --absent "Squat" --timeout 10
tap id:folder-more
wait id:folder-menu-move-history
tap id:folder-menu-move-history
wait id:move-history-sheet
expect "Moved to Training" id:move-history-clear id:move-history-undo-0 --absent id:move-history-undo-1
look 05_history
tap id:move-history-undo-0
expect "Undone" --absent id:move-history-undo-0 --timeout 10
tap id:move-history-clear
wait id:sheet-confirm
tap id:sheet-confirm
wait id:sheet-confirm --gone
expect "No recent moves" --absent id:move-history-clear --timeout 5
key back
wait id:move-history-sheet --gone
expect "Squat" "Deadlift" --timeout 10
errors
