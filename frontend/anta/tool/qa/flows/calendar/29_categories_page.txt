# The Categories page end to end, as it stands before its rework
# (docs/calendar-categories-roadmap.md §7 S0): the seeded Strength is
# archived through its eye and stays listed, its ⋮ then offers Show, and the
# ⋮'s archive item restores it, after which the ⋮ offers Hide again; it is
# dragged below Archive by a long press and put back, the order asserted both
# ways; deleted through its ⋮ with the confirm, which names its one seeded
# event, the message waited for and the row gone; then a category is created
# through + and listed.
# - Reached as flow 18 reaches it. Strength is the tenth row, so it is
#   scrolled to before its eye is tapped.
# - Both rows stay listed whether the archive landed or not, so the archive
#   and the restore are read from the row's ⋮: its archive item says Show on
#   an archived category and Hide on a listed one. The eye's tap and the
#   restore hold 500 ms, because the menu builds its items when it opens and
#   would show the flag from before an unfinished write.
# - `expect "Show" --absent "Hide"` relies on the popup's barrier keeping the
#   page's nodes out of the tree: Archive's eye also says "Show" and every
#   listed row's eye says "Hide". If the barrier let them through, the
#   --absent would fail.
# - The drag lifts the row by a long press anywhere on it (the delayed drag
#   listener lifts at 500 ms), so each drag holds 900 ms. Archive, the last
#   row, is scrolled to first so both rows are on screen for the drag. The
#   first drag holds 500 ms afterwards so its reorder write and refresh land
#   before the second drag starts.
# - The order is asserted by `expect --ordered` on the rows' ids, the dump's
#   traversal order: Strength before Archive, Archive first after the drag,
#   Strength first again once it is dragged back.
# - The delete's confirm is the shared dialog's id:sheet-confirm.
# - The new category is appended after every other row: it is scrolled to
#   before it is expected.
