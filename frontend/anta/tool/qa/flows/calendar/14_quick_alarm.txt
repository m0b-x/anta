# The quick alarm (docs/calendar-language-tier-2-roadmap.md §3.4): a long
# press on today, the picker's Alarm… row, the sheet on its ids — the time
# row into the pad and out through its ✕, a preset, a typed title, Type to
# Reminder and back — then Save, the snackbar and its Undo, which deletes the
# event again; the Alerts hub is where the deletion is read.
# - Nothing here asserts the time or the day: the sheet opens on the next
#   quarter hour, "In 20 min" lands tomorrow in the last minutes of a day
#   (and the grid then selects tomorrow, so the flow ends on Go to today),
#   and Tonight is switched off (in place) once 21:00 has passed.
# - The sheet opens on Alarm; the quick alarm has no full-screen warning, so
#   the line under the chips names the tier on both.
# - The snackbar stays three seconds: Undo is the step right after it shows.
# - The saved alarm's row may sit below the day panel's fold, where a node
#   counts as absent. The hub lists the soonest alert first, so "Tea timer"
#   absent there means the event and its alert are gone.
longpress "{{longdate}}"
wait id:quick-alarm-row
tap id:quick-alarm-row
wait id:quick-alarm-save
expect id:quick-alarm-close id:quick-alarm-time id:quick-alarm-preset-20 id:quick-alarm-preset-60 id:quick-alarm-preset-tonight id:quick-alarm-name id:quick-alarm-type-reminder id:quick-alarm-type-alarm id:quick-alarm-remove-after "Plays on the alarm stream until you stop or snooze it."
shot 14_quick_alarm
tap id:quick-alarm-time
wait id:time-pad-done
tap id:time-pad-cancel
wait id:quick-alarm-save
tap id:quick-alarm-preset-20
tap id:quick-alarm-name
type "Tea timer" --replace
tap id:quick-alarm-type-reminder
expect "Sits in the shade with Snooze and Done. Silent mode and Focus apply." "Tea timer"
shot 14_quick_alarm_reminder
tap id:quick-alarm-type-alarm
expect "Plays on the alarm stream until you stop or snooze it."
tap id:quick-alarm-save
wait "UNDO"
expect "Tea timer set for"
shot 14_quick_alarm_set

wait "UNDO" --gone
tap id:calendar-more
wait id:calendar-alerts-open
tap id:calendar-alerts-open
wait id:calendar-add-event --gone
expect "Alerts" --absent "Tea timer"
