# The removed-holidays sheet (docs/calendar-language-tier-3-roadmap.md §3.6)
# from Calendar settings: the seeded suppressed Christmas Day (on a relative
# day, {{day+60}}) in its read row with the Restore button by id, the
# restore, the message over the sheet and the empty caption, then ✕.
# - This flow consumes the seed: the restored day is a built-in that no
#   longer falls on a holiday, so it cannot be removed again from the day
#   panel. A lone re-run needs `qa relaunch --fresh --seed`.
# - The overlay bar is drawn 80 dp above the screen's bottom, which on this
#   one-row sheet is the header: ✕ is tapped once the bar has left.
tap id:calendar-more
wait id:calendar-settings-open
tap id:calendar-settings-open
wait id:calendar-add-event --gone
tap "Removed holidays"
wait id:holiday-restore-christmasDay-{{day+60}}
expect id:removed-holidays-close "Christmas Day" --absent "No holidays removed"
shot 21_removed_holidays
tap id:holiday-restore-christmasDay-{{day+60}}
wait "Holiday restored"
expect "No holidays removed" --absent "Christmas Day"
shot 21_removed_holidays_empty
wait "Holiday restored" --gone
tap id:removed-holidays-close
wait "Search settings"
key back
wait id:calendar-add-event
errors
