# From the root a fresh launch lands on to the calendar through the drawer;
# `qa pass calendar` runs this after every reseed, before the flow's steps.
# - The bottom panel restores its mode (Day / Timeline / Upcoming) from the
#   settings after it is on screen, and that restore overwrites a mode
#   tapped before it lands: a flow whose first step is `tap "Upcoming"` was
#   put back on Day. Nothing on screen marks the restore, so the last step
#   taps the already selected Day (which changes and persists nothing) and
#   holds 1 s; measured 2026-10-23: with no hold 4 of 4 runs went red, at
#   1.5 s and at 1 s flows 10 and 17 (which tap a mode first) were green
#   10 of 10 over five runs each on the fleet.
tap id:nav-menu
wait id:drawer-calendar
tap id:drawer-calendar
wait id:calendar-add-event
tap id:panel-mode-day --settle 1000
