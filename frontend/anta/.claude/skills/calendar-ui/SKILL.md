# Calendar UI

Every calendar surface speaks the app's UI language, and the calendar is where it was set (the 2026-09-25 event editor redesign — [docs/event-editor-redesign-roadmap.md](../../../docs/event-editor-redesign-roadmap.md) §2–§3 holds the owner's decisions D1–D20 and the token spec). **The rulebook is the `ui-language` skill; load it first.** The idioms, the row primitives, the sheet chrome, the metrics and the tests every surface ships with are there. This skill holds only what is the calendar's own.

**Adoption.** `docs/calendar-language-adoption-roadmap.md` (2026-09-27, the owner's max-effort plan for every calendar surface still outside the language, four tiers) is being worked, and the drive-by rule of `ui-language` is suspended while it runs: read it before touching any calendar sheet or page, and update its §9 status table per tier. §9 is also where to read which calendar surfaces speak the language today.

## Shared pieces

- **`EventAvatar(icon:, color:)`** — radius 20, icon 24, background at α 0.16. The colour follows `EventSummaryProvider.colorFor` (the custom colour only while `tintIcon`, else the category colour). The **bar** rule (`colorValue ?? category`) is a different thing and belongs to grid bars, rails and stripes.
- **`AgendaPeriodNav`** — chevron | title + count | a fixed 48 dp today slot (`FormMetrics.trailingButtonSize`) | chevron. Since D33 (2026-10-17) the title is 17 / 500 (`FormMetrics.headerTitleSize`) and the count the caption role, 13 / 400 `onSurfaceVariant` on the 18 px caption line (`FormMetrics.captionLineHeight`; `labelSmall` 11 and a w600 title before), its paddings and 24 px glyphs are named `FormMetrics.periodNav*`, the title's ink is `chipRadius`, and the title carries an Android `hint` with the tooltip's words as `CalendarViewMenu` does. `onTitleTap` makes the title a control (drop-down glyph) and is called with the title's own context: the day list opens `MonthYearPickerSheet` with it, the overview its Period menu through `showFormMenu(alignment: FormMenuAlignment.center)` anchored to that context (2026-10-14; centred under the title since D33 — the row rule `end` pinned the menu's right edge to the title's and left the menu at the screen's left edge). Null callbacks disable; the fixed slot is what keeps the title from shifting. The title may take two lines (`FormMetrics.periodTitleMaxLines`) in a box as tall as the tallest title the row will show — pass `month:` on a month nav so the box is measured over that year's twelve titles and paging never moves the grid; a year nav passes nothing. The twelve are laid out once per key and kept in a static `LruCache` (`yearTitleCacheSize`) keyed on every input of their layout, so a rebuild lays out only the shown title — a new input to that layout joins the key. The count is a semantics node of its own and shrinks to fit one line inside a box of its one-line height, never cut and never taller. `previousIdentifier` / `nextIdentifier` / `todayIdentifier` / `titleIdentifier` are optional ids, set only where a flow walks the nav (the day list's `day-list-nav-*`).
- **`YearMonthTile` + `MonthDotMatrix`** — the year overview's tiles, shared by the drill-down and the Dates sheet's year view. `YearMonthTile.gridDelegate` is the grid; `dayColors` paints per-day colours, `missedMask` / `dayMissedAlpha` fade missed days. One `Semantics` node per tile, the matrix excluded. Since D33 (2026-10-17) a tile's background is the host's: `AgendaYearTileBuilder.of(background:)`, and `AgendaYearGrid` / `AgendaYearPager` / `AgendaYearScroll` take `tileBackground`; every host on `pageGround` (the overview, the day list sheet, the Dates sheet's year view) passes `rowGroup` — one tone off its ground in both themes, as the row cards are (the token `surfaceContainerHigh` it painted before sat darker than the light ground); its radius, padding, gap, delegate numbers and two text sizes are `OverviewListMetrics.yearTile*`. Since 2026-10-15 the overview's Year mode draws them in `AgendaYearScroll` (one section per year under a flat 48 dp `_YearHeader`: the year at 22 / 500, the count at the right in the caption role, blank while unresolved; `OverviewListMetrics.yearHeaderHeight` measured once per text scale, `yearSectionGap` 16) and the day list sheet in `AgendaYearPager`; both through `AgendaYearTileBuilder`.
- **Day grids** render `CalendarDayCell` + `CalendarDayBars`, never table_calendar's own decorations. Dates go through `CalendarDatePickerSheet`, times through `TimePadSheet`, categories through `CategoryPickerSheet`, month/year through `MonthYearPickerSheet` — never `showDatePicker` or `showTimePicker`.
