# Sheet chrome

Reference, not rules — moved verbatim from the ui-language SKILL.md on 2026-10-11. Read only the part the task needs.

## Sheet chrome — the three shapes

**A form sheet** (`EventEditorSheet.show`, `EventDescriptionSheet.show`, `EventTemplateEditorSheet.show`) — for a sheet with typed text or a docked bar: `showModalBottomSheet(isScrollControlled: true, showDragHandle: false, enableDrag: false, backgroundColor: Colors.transparent, elevation: 0)` → `FractionallySizedBox(heightFactor: FormMetrics.sheetHeightFactor)` → **`FormSheetFrame(chrome: Column[FormSheetHandle, FormSheetHeader], body: [Expanded(SingleChildScrollView(padding: groupInset, bodyTop, groupInset, bodyBottom + clearance)), …a fixed footer], onLeave: _leave, isClean: () => !_isDirty, onDismiss: _popDiscarding)`** (2026-09-27, Tier 1 — never a copy of its drag code inside a sheet). The frame owns the drag because the route's own drag pops without consulting `PopScope`; its `PopScope(canPop: false)` routes ✕, back, the system back gesture and the barrier through the sheet's one `_leave()`, which asks (`AppDialogs.confirmDiscard`) only when the form is dirty — a fingerprint of every field in the editor, the text against what `loadText` left in the description sheet; a clean fling pops through `onDismiss`, a dirty one snaps back and asks. The header's hairline is a `ValueNotifier<bool>`, never `setState`; a new sheet takes it from `FormHeaderHairline` (`scrolled` to the header, `watch(child:)` around the scroll view, `dispose`), which hears the scroll metrics change as well as the scroll — a body that gets shorter (the keyboard closing) corrects its offset without telling a controller's listener, and the hairline would stay on over a body that no longer scrolls. **Large text (2026-10-24):** `FormSheetHeader` is one 48 dp row — ✕, title, action — at every scale by default, and a two-word title ellipsizes at 2.0 in German ("Ereigni…", found by the goldens). The opt-in `stackTitleAtLargeText` keeps the ✕ and the action on their 48 dp row and gives the title the full width below whenever the title's single line does not fit the inline slot (a slotted render box, `_RenderStackingHeader`, measures it against the action's laid-out width — a fit decision, never a scale threshold, because Android's "1.5" is 1.38× at 17 px), `headerTitleMaxLines` (2) lines, `headerStackedTitleBottomPadding` (8) under it — the Material large-top-app-bar shape; `OrdinalSortKey`s keep the screen-reader order ✕, title, action. Only `EventEditorSheet` opts in: seven large-text suites pin the one-row header at 2.0 for the other sheets, so making it the default is one app-wide decision (flip the default, rewrite those assertions).

**A sub-sheet** (`EventLookSheet.show`, `EventRepeatSheet.show`, the read-only `EventDetailSheet.show`, the four filter surfaces, the agenda filters sheet, the time pad, the month/year picker, the template picker, the sound sheet, the alert sheet and its Custom sub-sheet, the quick alarm): `showModalBottomSheet(isScrollControlled: true, useSafeArea: true, showDragHandle: false, backgroundColor: pageGround, shape: top radius sheetRadius)` → `ConstrainedBox(maxHeight: screen height × factor)` → `Column(mainAxisSize: min)` of `FormSheetHandle`, `FormSheetHeader(close · title · FormHeaderTextButton Done)`, `Flexible(SingleChildScrollView(…))`. Unguarded: a draft costs one tap to redo.

**The form sheet's box without its guard** (`CalendarDatePickerSheet`, `IconPickerSheet` — Tier 1 D10 — and `AgendaDayListSheet`, Tier 3 D4) is for a *filler*: a body that is a grid, a matrix, a list under a live search, or a body that switches between views. The sub-sheet's route flags with `FractionallySizedBox(heightFactor: FormMetrics.sheetHeightFactor)` and `Column(stretch)` of handle, header, the body in an `Expanded` and an optional fixed footer; route drag, no `PopScope`, never `FormSheetFrame` — back and the scrim must dismiss a filler whatever state its body is in. A content-tall filler would change height between Month / Year / List or jump on every keystroke, and "nothing moves under the finger" outranks content-tall. **A filler may pin a band under its header** — the icon picker's search group, the day list's caption (`FormMetrics.headerCaptionBottomPadding` under it) and its chip row — that stays put while the body changes; **a filler's header carries no hairline** (with a band between the header and the scrolling body, a rule under the title reads as stray) and passes `trailingInset: FormMetrics.headerActionInset`. The Dates sheet's Today is a fixed 48 dp slot after the title in the month grid's own header row and in the year row (`AgendaPeriodNav`'s grammar), never a second header icon.

## Sheet chrome — the long rules for both shapes

- **Bottom clearance is the larger of `viewInsets.bottom` and `viewPadding.bottom`**, added to the scroll view's bottom padding, or to the fixed footer (a docked markdown bar, an action row) when one sits below the scroll view. Never to the whole body of a fixed-fraction sheet, and never a `const EdgeInsets`. The header is the first `Column` child so it stays tappable whatever the inset. **Add every new sheet to `test/widgets/sheet_bottom_clearance_test.dart`** — this defect has shipped five times and that test is the only thing that has caught it. Read both insets through `MediaQuery`, never from `View.of(context)`: the app-root `KeyboardInsetGuard` is what keeps Android's stale keyboard inset out of every sheet, and a sheet needs no stale-inset guard of its own.
- **A live-apply sheet** — every control writes at once and dismissing discards nothing (the fasting schedule and style sheets, removed holidays, the palette) — has ✕ with the `close` tooltip, not `cancel`, and an empty trailing slot (`SizedBox.shrink()`, `trailingInset: FormMetrics.headerActionInset`): there is nothing to confirm.
- **A message raised from inside a sheet goes through `OverlaySnackbar`**, never `CustomSnackbar`: a Scaffold's snackbar is drawn on the page *under* the modal route, where nobody sees it ("Template saved" was invisible until 2026-10-03, and so, until Tier 3, were the category editor's save failure, the palette's messages, the colour picker's "Copied", "Holiday restored" and the swatch strip's two refusals inside any sheet that embeds it). The overlay bar is a live region and cancels its own timer. Its box takes taps only on its text line and its ✕ (a transparent `Material` over a `PhysicalModel`, 2026-10-13): the coloured `Material` it had before absorbed every tap inside the box, and on a short sub-sheet that strip — 76 dp tall, 80 dp above the bottom — covered the header's ✕ right after "Saved as …", so the overview's views sheet could not be closed until the message timed out. The text stays tappable because nine suites pin that a finger can reach the message. **Its look is the theme's, not its own** (master record decision 9, 2026-10-17): until then both bars painted `inverseSurface` — `#E6E0E9` in the dark theme, a near-white card the owner saw on the overview — so `AppTheme._themeFor` declares one `SnackBarThemeData` (`menuSurface`, `onSurface` text at the menu label's size, `FormMetrics.menuRadius` with an `outlineVariant` hairline, the menu's elevation, the action in `primary`, the ✕ in `onSurfaceVariant`); `CustomSnackbar` inherits it and `OverlaySnackbar` reads its colours and shape from `Theme.of(context).snackBarTheme`, so the two bars cannot drift and a palette change reaches both.
- **Stale semantics after a resize under a leaving route** (2026-10-13, found by the overview's views flow): `await showDialog(...)` resumes while the dialog's barrier is still animating out and its `BlockSemantics` still blocks the sheet; a sheet that grows or shrinks in that window (a row added after "Save", removed after "Delete") has its geometry pass skipped for the blocked branch, and once unblocked nothing marks it dirty — `_RenderObjectSemantics._updateGeometry` rebuilds a node only when its size or visibility changed, so the ✕ and every row keep the transform they had before the resize until their next layout. The app's tree is wrong, not only the harness's dump: TalkBack focuses where the ✕ was. `FormSheetSemanticsRefresh` (`form_sheet_frame.dart`) listens to the sheet route's `secondaryAnimation` and, when the route above reaches `dismissed`, walks the sheet's render subtree once with `markNeedsSemanticsUpdate()`; `OverviewViewSheet.show` and `FilterPresetSheet.show` wrap their box in it, and `test/widgets/support/semantics_geometry.dart`'s `semanticsGlobalRect` is how a test compares a node's reported rect with its painted one (within 1 px after three frames). A plain `setState` resize with nothing above the sheet is fresh and needs no refresh.

## Shared pieces

- **`ValueChangeHighlight(value:, child:)`** — wraps the row a pad or picker writes to, so an auto-closed entry is read back visibly.

## Dialogs — the two confirm styles

"The rule above" in this paragraph is the last of SKILL.md's rules the rows encode (Save, Done, the destructive `AlertDialog`).

`AppDialogs` (`lib/widgets/app_dialogs.dart`) is the one dialog layer, and a form sheet's leave guard is `AppDialogs.confirmDiscard`. **Two confirm styles exist and they disagree**: the rule above (a `TextButton` cancel and a `FilledButton.tonal` confirm — the editor's delete dialog and `confirmDiscard`) and `AppDialogs.confirm(isDestructive: true)`, which predates the language and draws an error-filled confirm under a 48 dp icon at every other delete in the app. Which of the two is the language's is an open app-wide decision (`docs/ui-language-adoption-roadmap.md`); until it is taken, a migrated surface keeps the dialog its old version showed and never adds a third style.
