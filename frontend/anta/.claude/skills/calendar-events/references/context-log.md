- Calendar bottom panel: `lib/widgets/calendar_bottom_panel.dart` owns the mode (`CalendarPanelMode`: day / timeline / upcoming) **and** the agenda filters below the page, so agenda-search keystrokes never rebuild the `TableCalendar` grid; an expand toggle collapses the grid (`AnimatedSize`, transient). **Since 2026-09-14 the panel paints `SurfaceRoles.pageGround` behind everything it hosts and its cards (`DaySummaryPanel`'s rows, every `_AgendaCard`) are `rowGroup`** — the browser's layering, one tone between card and ground in both themes. On plain `surface` the light card's default `surfaceContainerLow` was one tone away and barely separated; the grid above stays on `surface`, so in light it reads as a sheet over the panel, and in dark the ground *is* `surface` so only the cards lift. The timeline's blocks are 16 % tints and need nothing. Upcoming agenda: `lib/utils/event_agenda.dart` (pure range scan, clamped 366 days, **never inside `eventLoader`**) + `UpcomingAgendaFilters` (persisted via `SettingsService`, empty priority set = "all", query debounced 500 ms + flushed on dispose) + `AgendaListView` (memoized rows, holiday interleave, reuses `EventSummaryProvider`). The upcoming window **starts on today unless `followSelectedDay` is on** (default off, 2026-08-22 redesign — `docs/upcoming-agenda-redesign-roadmap.md`): the anchor is **panel-owned state** (`_CalendarBottomPanelState._agendaAnchor` → `UpcomingAgendaView.anchorDay`), not `selectedDay`, because the selection and the window are two different things. `_syncAnchor` holds every rule — following off ⇒ today (which also picks up a date rollover on the next rebuild); no `previous` ⇒ resolve from scratch (filters loaded, or the toggle just flipped on); a `CalendarSelectionSource.agendaRow` selection **never** re-anchors, so a row tap cannot truncate the list it was tapped in; anything else follows a selection that actually changed. `SelectCalendarDay` carries a **required** `CalendarSelectionSource` (`grid`/`agendaRow`/`navigation`, lib/models/calendar_selection_source.dart) so a new dispatch site has to decide; `CalendarPageLoaded.selectionSource` is in `props` but excluded from **both** `sameGridInputs` and `samePanelInputs` (nothing renders from it, and a source change that matters always rides with a `selectedDay` change). A custom range still overrides the anchor, the rescan stays guarded so a pinned range ignores anchor moves, and the anchor is not persisted (reopens on today); the header's `from <date>` chip (`upcomingAnchorFrom`, delete × = `upcomingResetAnchor`) shows only while the anchor drives the window and sits off today, and returns it. The Custom period chip is an `InputChip` whose delete × (`upcomingClearRange`) clears the range back to the anchored preset. **Anchor-rescan perf (2026-08 regression fix, `docs/calendar-anchor-perf-regression-2026-08.md`):** hot callers hold a normalized day and call `CalendarEvent.occursOnUtcDay` (cached `startDateUtc`/`endDateUtc`; `occursOn` is the normalizing wrapper; `EventSkips.isSkipped`/`EventPresence.isMissed` now require a date-only UTC day, debug-asserted; the model constructor is no longer `const`); the agenda scan prunes candidates by `[startDate,endDate]` span and buckets one-time events (exempting `SpecificDatesRecurrence`, which has no pre-start guard); the panel `BlocBuilder` gates on `CalendarPageLoaded.samePanelInputs` (all inputs except `focusedDay`/`format`) and `_onChangeFocusedDay` has a no-op guard, so month-paging no longer re-runs the scan; a query-only change debounces the rescan ~200 ms and an anchor rescan scrolls the agenda to top; row formatters are cached per locale. **Agenda layers (2026-08):** events carry one mutually-exclusive `AgendaEventType` (all/recurring/oneTime/**none**) applied in `occurrencesInRange`'s candidate pre-filter (`none` short-circuits to no events — that is "holidays without events"); holidays + fasting are independent day-annotation toggles interleaved by `buildAgendaRows`' three-cursor merge (`EventAgenda.fastingDaysInRange` reuses `FastingSummaryProvider`, hidden unless `FastingCalendar.isEnabled`; priority row hides when eventType none). A category **allowlist** (`UpcomingAgendaFilters.categoryIds`, empty = all, sorted-CSV) narrows events in the same candidate pre-filter, composed on top of the inherited `hiddenCategoryIds` (both apply). `collapseRecurring` (off by default) post-filters the scan `result` to one row per recurring event (its next occurrence; one-time untouched) in **two passes** — tally per recurring id, then keep the first-seen occurrence carrying that tally as `EventOccurrence.occurrenceCountInWindow`, rendered as a "N× in window" badge — leaving `occursOn` counts intact; post-filter, never a scan short-circuit, or the occursOn-budget and anchor tests break. **Event presentation (2026-08-23)** is `UpcomingAgendaFilters.eventDisplay` — an `AgendaEventDisplay` of `everyOccurrence`/`perEvent`/`summary`, default **`everyOccurrence`** — the events layer's twin of the fasting axis, replacing the `collapseRecurring` bool for the same reason (three mutually exclusive presentations; a second flag would have encoded "collapse *and* summarize"). Persisted under `calendar_upcoming_event_display` with a **read-time** fallback to the retired `calendar_upcoming_collapse_recurring` bool (`true` → `perEvent`, `false` → `everyOccurrence`, never written again, no migration). Condensing events is **entirely opt-in**: an install that never touched it opens on exactly the rows it always had. `perEvent` is the shipped collapse post-filter. **`summary`** is one card per **category** — a category is to events what a tradition is to fasting — built by `EventAgenda.categorySummariesOf`, which folds the scan's own output into one `EventCategorySummary` per category (its occurrences, the count of **distinct** events behind them, first/last day). It is a **post-pass costing zero `occursOn` calls** and can only describe occurrences the scan already produced, so the card can never disagree with its own drill-down; the scan runs **uncollapsed** in this mode because the card wants both numbers and the collapse pass would discard the occurrence tally. Categories order by first in-window occurrence. The subtitle leads with distinct events (a daily event across ninety days is one event, not ninety), adds the occurrence tally only when it exceeds that count, then the range — "12 events · 47× in window · Aug 23 – Sep 21". `AgendaEventSummaryRow` is **not** an `AgendaEntryRow` (entry counts exclude it, like both siblings), and in summary mode `buildAgendaRows` walks an **empty occurrence cursor** — the cards are a fold of the same list, so leaving the rows in would show everything twice. **Summary cards sort on `(priority, insertion index)`, never priority alone**: `List.sort` is not stable in Dart and cards legitimately share a band (every event category sits at 0, two fasting traditions share 160), so a bare priority sort let identical input reshuffle between rebuilds; events lead, then the holiday card (150), then fasting (160), matching the day panel. **The drill-down can edit (2026-08-23):** `AgendaDayListEntry.onEdit` is an optional trailing action — event rows open the editor with it, a holiday or fasting day has nothing to open and leaves it null (which also keeps those rows free of an empty action strip) — and the sheet **resolves an intent rather than running it** (`Future<({DateTime? focusDay, VoidCallback? edit})?>`), so the editor opens after the sheet has closed instead of stacked on it, the same contract `EventDetailSheet` follows. The sheet stays dumb: no facade reads, and it localizes only its own chrome (the pencil's tooltip included).

 **Event search is a parsed query, not a needle (2026-08-24):** `lib/utils/event_search_query.dart` owns the grammar — `EventSearchQuery.parse(raw, {localeName})` splits on whitespace, folds each token once through the shared `normalizeForSearch`, dedupes into `terms` (a repeated word is one constraint) capped at `maxTerms = 30` and addressed by an `int` bitmask, and groups them into `clauses` each carrying an optional `EventSearchDate`. `parse('')` returns the `const empty` — zero allocation, `satisfied` trivially true — so an empty query costs exactly what it did before. An occurrence on day `D` matches iff **every** clause is satisfied, and a clause is satisfied when either all its text terms appear in the title / the description resolved for `D` / the **localized category label** (clause-mates may be answered by different fields), **or** its date matches `D`. So `aug 26` returns everything on Aug 26 *plus* anything whose text holds both "aug" and "26"; `gym aug 26` requires both clauses. Dates parse from localized month names (`DateFormat.MMMM(locale).dateSymbols`, all four symbol lists, folded, punctuation-stripped, unique ≥3-char prefix, cached per locale), day+month in both orders (`aug 26` / `26 aug` / `26. august`), and ISO validated by a UTC round-trip — **never** `DateTime.tryParse` (rollover, same reason `fasting_schedule.dart:261` warns). Bare numbers, bare years and impossible dates (`2026-02-30`, `feb 30`, `aug 44`) degrade to plain text. **`EventAgenda` stays context-free**: the view resolves a `Map<String, String>` id→localized label and passes it in — a `Set` of matched ids cannot express *which* term a category answered, which AND semantics needs. Per-event state is one `_EventQueryMatch` (`base` = title|category, `template` folded **only** for what `base` left open, plus `varies`/`always`), so a title hit still costs no description fold, and `matchesQuery` is one map lookup plus an `always` short-circuit per (event, day); a live per-occurrence override still **replaces** the template contribution, empty included. The query is parsed once per rescan (`_syncQuery`, no-op when text+locale stand still) and category labels are re-resolved on `CalendarCategories.revision`, so a rename behind the panel's back is picked up. Holidays and fasting run the **same** clause test against their own localized labels instead of their old private `contains`, so all three layers agree on what "matches" means. **Search coverage, reach and language (2026-08-25):** the governing rule is now *if a row displays a piece of text, typing that text finds the row* — the scan used to fold only title|category|description while rows displayed recurrence patterns, times, "All day", priority words, regime names, "Public holiday" and cards titled "Holidays"/"Orthodox". `occurrencesInRange` gained a lazy `labelTextOf` closure (built by the view, so `EventAgenda` stays `AppLocalizations`-free), invoked **once per candidate event** and only when `base` has not settled the query, folded **before** the description so a label hit costs no description fold; `AgendaSearchText.forEvent` composes exactly what `EventSummaryProvider._subtitleFor` renders, and `EventAgenda.debugLabelFolds` pins the budget. `countLabel` ("Week 3", "26 years") is **knowingly excluded** — it is per-(event, day) and would break the `always` short-circuit every budget depends on. A non-empty query with no pinned custom range **auto-widens** the window to `maxRangeDays` (366): the reported "`lent` finds nothing" was never a string bug — English "Great Lent" contains "lent" — it was that search filtered the 30-day window instead of finding across the year. `_fastingMatches` folds the override **and** the period name (OR, never the display's `??`, which made renaming a period unsearchable), plus the tradition name (folded nowhere before, though it titles the summary card), the regime and the style description; `_recomputeHolidays` folds `dayBarPublicHoliday` + the `upcomingShowHolidays` card title once per scan. Cross-language terms come from **hidden localized keywords** (`FastingCalendar.traditionKeywordsOf` / `searchKeywordsOf`, nullable for minor periods) on the settings search's proven `swipeKeywords` pattern — matched, never rendered, each locale carrying the other languages' terms. On a **zero-result** query only, `FuzzyRank` (`lib/utils/fuzzy_rank.dart` — the vocabulary bar's 4-tier prefix › word-start › substring › subsequence scorer, extracted unchanged and now shared, `VocabularyMatcher` delegating to it) ranks a catalogue of titles, category labels, enabled traditions/periods and the window's holidays into up to three "did you mean" chips that replace the query. Fuzziness **suggests only** — the filtering grammar stays exact substring.

- Event alerts, **the lock screen (2026-10-04; record `docs/event-alerts-os-integration-roadmap.md` §13)**. No perpetual animation: the suite `pumpAndSettle`s and the QA agent settles frames before every verb. **The page closes its own route** — `pop` when it is on top, `removeRoute` from underneath — because two alarms due in one minute stack two pages (the plugin sounds one and holds the other, and tells the app of both within a second), and a plain pop from the lower one took the other ring's page and left itself behind, dead. **`AlarmGround`** (`lib/widgets/alarm_ground.dart`) is what the page and `LockScreenCurtain` both stand on: `pageGround` under one `AnnotatedRegion` that makes both system bars transparent with icons by theme. **It hands the navigation bar back** when the last ground leaves the screen (a count, applied in a microtask): Android keeps a bar's style until another is set and no other page sets one for the navigation bar, so a bare region would strip the contrast band from every page after a ring. It hands it back **in the state the app has at launch** — what `MaterialApp` itself sets, read off `SystemUiOverlayStyle.light` (colour `0xFF000000`, light buttons), over the transparent divider, with contrast enforcement on (`AlarmGround.barsAfter`, `test/widgets/alarm_ground_test.dart`) — not by the phone's brightness: from Android 15 Flutter's embedding no longer passes a bar colour on and the phone forces the buttons of a light window dark, which is how a hand-back by brightness looked right on the API 36 emulator while, below 15, it left dark buttons on a light band under every page after the first ring. **Keep has three moments** (`AlertRingController.keep`): from the press `willRemoveEvent` is false, so no Stop removes the event; `keepEvent` — what the page says "kept" from — turns true only once the upsert has returned, or when `load()` finds the event already kept; a write that throws, or finds no event to write to, takes both back. **Stop decides the removal at the press and may only narrow it** (`AlertRingController.stop()`): it removes only if `willRemoveEvent` was true at the press and still is at the end, so a Keep whose write fails while Stop is in flight does not widen it back. The write reaches the calendar through **`AlertKeepNotice`** (`lib/services/alert_keep_notice.dart`, a process-global flag with no payload and no freshness window — a reload owed an hour ago is still the right reload): `CalendarPage` answers with `LoadCalendarEvents`, and a calendar mounted by *Open event* right after a Keep holds its initial target until that reload has replaced the event list. A detail sheet already open when the Keep lands holds its own copy of the event and saves it whole, which would write the flag back: so after a Keep the calendar's detail-sheet trip (`_detailSheetLoop`, `afterKeep`) takes the remove-after flag from the bloc's copy before it writes its own copy back or hands it to the editor, and every calendar on the navigator counts a Keep it hears (`_keepsHeard`) — the notice is one flag that only the first calendar to hear it takes. Guards: `test/widgets/alarm_page_test.dart` through `AlarmPageRobot` (a redraw rewrites the robot, never a test body; its layout matrix is en / de / ro × 1.0 / 1.3 / 2.0 × two phone sizes × both themes — the test font is about twice Roboto's width, so whether a label fits one line is the emulator's to show), `test/controllers/alert_ring_controller_test.dart`, `test/widgets/alarm_ground_test.dart`, `test/services/alert_keep_notice_test.dart`, `test/widgets/calendar_keep_notice_test.dart`. **One module writes every notification's words**: `alertNotificationContent(kind, payload, strings, {dayLabel, untilLabel, linkedNote})` in `lib/utils/alert_notification_content.dart`, pure. The title is the event's title, always (the tier word when it is empty, "Test alarm" for a test ring); the text is what the notification is and the time it is about, in the hub's own words — `{kind} · {time}` with the event's own time for an alert that is firing ("Alarm · 18:00 – 19:30", "Reminder · 18:00", "Snoozed · …" — and, for an occurrence on another day, its day first, "Reminder · Mon, Sep 21 · 18:00" (`alertsDayThenWhen`, since 2026-10-10), because the one-line row and the heads-up cut the end of the text, where the day used to be) and the alarm's own for one that was missed ("Missed · 06:50"), "Alarm at {time}" on the upcoming notice, "Until {time}" or "Session in progress" on the chip; expanded, the description's first line is added under the text and never replaces it; the action that settles comes first (Stop · Snooze N min; Done · Snooze N min; Skip · Open; Done · Open note). Nothing in the gateway writes a title or a text inline. `AndroidAlertGateway` cannot be built in a test (`AlertAvailability` gates its registration), so **everything it decides is a pure top-level function beside it** that it only calls — a details function per kind (`alertReminderDetails`, `alertNoticeDetails`, `alertMissedDetails`, `alertFallbackAlarmDetails`), `alertRingSettings` for the plugin's own notification, `sessionChipArguments` for `SessionChip.kt`, `snoozedRepost` / `snoozedRepostFor` / `snoozedRepostTitleOnly` for the background isolate, `ringReportPayload`, `alertArmContext`, the avatar's painter and file name — pinned by `test/utils/alert_notification_content_test.dart` and `test/services/android_alert_notifications_test.dart` (`ringReportPayload` by `test/services/android_alert_gateway_test.dart`); what has to stay inside the class is held as text by the source guards under `test/android/`. **The payload carries optional facts, and absence means the old behaviour for good.** `AlertPayload` gained `fireAtMs` (the due instant), `whenLabel` (the event's own time as a line, empty when it would only repeat the due time), `tintValue` and `glyphKey` (the avatar, resolved when the entry was armed), `languageCode`, `iconFile`, `grouped` and `reRing`; each is left out of the wire form while it says nothing and every reader keeps the fallback it had before the field existed, because another database's entries and standing snoozes are never re-armed — a payload written without them can ring years later. A new fact the page or the isolate needs is one more field of that kind, never a required one and never a database read. "Snoozed" is said only where it is known (`reRing`, set on the ring report when this process saw the plugin's snooze move, or a snooze taken on the page) — never from `snooze` alone, which is true for every test ring. **Nothing formats a time or a date in the background isolate**, which has no date symbols loaded and no database, and where a throw loses a snooze: every clock time and day name an entry shows is written once, when it is armed, on the UI isolate, by **`AlertLabels`** (`lib/utils/alert_labels.dart`) — built once per pass from the app's language and the phone's clock style, read by the scheduler through `AlertGateway.labels` (`AlertLabels.fixed`, 24-hour English, on the no-op binding and in every suite), every method total; the pass reads the language through `SettingsService.getLocaleCode()` and **never** through `AppDatabase.getInstance()`, which re-resolves which database is active and carries out a stored switch (a rename of the open database, a switch waiting behind its exit dialog) under the running app — the binding's direct read happens at its first start only (`test/android/alerts_channel_test.dart`) — and the isolate's re-post only places strings: it takes them from `alertGatewayStrings(payload.languageCode)`, composes **before** it cancels, and posts title-only if composing throws. **The avatar is a file.** Every kind but the ring wears the event's colour and its avatar (the ring is colorized, so colour alone): `EventAvatar`'s own proportions at `kAlertLargeIconDp` × the device pixel ratio, one PNG per glyph, colour, size and way of drawing under `<application support>/alert_icons/` (the name carries the disc's alpha and the glyph's size, `…_<pixels>_a<alpha>_g<glyph size>.png`, because a file found under a name is never drawn again), handed over as `FilePathAndroidBitmap` — a scheduled notification is kept as JSON until it is due, and a snoozed re-post hands the path on from an isolate that cannot draw. The files are never cleared (a standing reminder holds the path), the raster and the write are bounded (`_rasterPatience`), and a failure posts without an icon: decor is never worth an alert. **Three channels of the app's own, and what posts on each** (ids fixed forever: a channel's importance and sound are frozen when it is created, while its name and description are re-created from the current strings at every start): `alerts_reminder` (high) — the reminder and its snoozed re-post; `alerts_quiet` (low, new in 2026-10; created with `playSound: false, enableVibration: false`, which both default to on and freeze with the importance, so a phone whose owner turns the channel up does not start to sound) — the upcoming notice, the Missed notice and the session chip; `alerts_alarm_v2` (max, alarm stream) — the dormant fallback alarm behind `kAlarmTierUsesNotifications`. The ring itself is the fork's notification on the plugin's own `alarm_plugin_channel`. The quiet kinds were moved because **Android 16 bundles an app's loose notifications as soon as two stand in one section of the shade**, flags each silent before it decides whether to alert, and posts a summary of its own on the channel that made the bundle: two quiet ones together sounded, and a reminder that fired beside one was silenced. A channel's importance picks the section, and in the silent one nothing sounds, alone or bundled, and nothing shares a bundle with a reminder; the three keep their `silent` flag under it. **A reminder carries a group key** (`kAlertGroupKey` with `GroupAlertBehavior.children`) on Android 16 and above — `alertRemindersGroupedOn`, `kAlertGroupMinSdk` 36, read once through the alerts channel's `sdkInt`; a read that fails arms that one reminder loose and asks again — because a notification that names a group is not bundled at post, so it sounds and peeks first. **Never a summary**: an app summary stays behind as a row when its last child leaves by a timeout or a native action, and taking it down takes the standing reminders with it. The isolate cannot ask which Android it runs on, so an entry armed in the group carries `grouped` and its re-post comes back in the same shape. Missed is dated by the alarm it reports (`when` is the due instant). `alerts_quiet` is deliberately not in the permission gateway's channel list — turned off, it costs no alert — and the channel itself is the one decision of the rework to confirm with the owner before a release, since its importance is frozen on every phone that creates it. Seen on 2026-10-10 on the API 36 emulator: a reminder behind a PIN keyguard posts and sounds, every kind reads as above from `dumpsys notification`, and a reminder for an occurrence on another day keeps its day in the 360 dp one-line row. Not seen, and owed before a release: the promoted chip on a low channel, a second platform build (the reminder's shape leans on the three-second wait before the platform bundles), Android below 16, a clean install, a real phone.
