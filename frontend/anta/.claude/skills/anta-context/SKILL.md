# ANTA map

CLAUDE.md is in your context: the architecture flow, the non-negotiable rules, the commands, the OS you are on. This skill is the "where": the entry points per area and the reference files that hold each area's facts. Read a reference only for the part the task needs; `overview.md` holds the product framing when a decision needs it.

## Entry points per area

- **Folder/note browser**: `lib/pages/optimized_folder_content_page.dart` — nested folders, pagination, sorting, manual reorder, swipe actions, move history, selection bar. Its rows are the browser's content rows (`lib/widgets/content_rows.dart`, the `ui-language` skill).
- **Note editor**: `lib/pages/optimized_note_editor_page.dart` with six page-owned controllers under `lib/controllers/` — `EditorSettingsController`, `NoteEditorPositionController`, `NoteSaveCoordinator`, `EditorEditTracker` (paste reflow, Enter list continuation, `runGuarded` for programmatic inserts), `NoteEditorStatsTracker`, `EditorRenderController` — plain classes and `ChangeNotifier`s, never a BLoC on the typing path; the wrapper's tap/Tab/ghost rules are the pure `EditorInputPolicy` in `lib/utils/`. The page file keeps the widget, the lifecycle and `build`; the rest of its State is a chain of mixins in parts under `lib/pages/optimized_note_editor_page/` (`state, preview, typing, inputs, mount, loading, sharing, remote, note_actions, taps, toolbar, views`). Rendering rules: the `markdown-engine` skill.
- **Toolbar and shortcuts**: `lib/widgets/markdown_bar.dart`; `markdown_settings_page.dart`, `shortcut_editor_page.dart`, `note_bar_assignment_page.dart`. Counter bindings on a shortcut: `CustomMarkdownShortcut.counters` (max 2) with `{c1}` / `{c2}` tokens expanded by `ShortcutApplier`; keep the legacy `counterId` populated when `insertType == 'counter'`.
- **Counters**: `lib/services/counter_service.dart` (cache, debounced writes, pinning, ordering), `counter_management_page.dart`, `counter_per_note_page.dart`. Global counter values use `noteId == ''` in `counter_values`; respect `scope`, `isPinned` and ordering.
- **Auto-save**: `lib/services/auto_save_service.dart` — debounce, interval saves, lifecycle flushes, retries; the page's `NoteSaveCoordinator` owns early create and duplicate titles.
- **Backup**: `lib/services/backup_service.dart` — versioned JSON; every new persisted field defaults when absent so old backups keep importing.
- **Import/export**: `lib/services/import_export_service.dart`; UI only through `ImportExportBloc`; exports through `shareExport`; `createX` stamps now, `importX` preserves; an archive version bump accepts the previous version — `references/import-export.md`.
- **Multi-database**: `lib/services/database_manager.dart`; every DB-backed singleton follows the `DatabaseLifecycle` reset contract (the `drift-migrations` skill).
- **Navigation and launch restore**: a persisted stack of `NavDestination`s (`lib/models/nav_destination.dart`) recorded by `NavigationHistoryObserver` / `NavigationHistoryService` off the route stamps `AppNavigator` puts in `RouteSettings.arguments`, replayed by `AppNavigator.restoreLastLocation()` from `main.dart`. Keep the existence checks — never navigate to a deleted folder or note — and never await a push during replay. The whole design: `references/navigation.md`. **The guides** (2026-11-08, `docs/guide-hub-roadmap.md`): the drawer's How to use ANTA row opens `GuideHubPage` (`lib/pages/guide_hub_page.dart`, one search over every guide), whose rows open `CalendarGuidePage` or `GuideBookPage(book)` (notes, sync, app) → `GuideTopicPage`; the catalogues are `lib/models/notes_guide_content.dart` and `app_guide_content.dart` over the `GuideTopic` / `GuideEntry` types in `guide_content.dart`, the shared hub body `lib/widgets/guide_hub_list.dart`, the example row `guide_example_row.dart` (source + `SimpleMarkdownPreview`); keys `guideHub*` / `guideBook*` / `notesGuide*` / `appGuide*` with braces as `{open}`/`{close}` placeholders; ids `guide-hub`, `guide-hub-book-<book>`, `guide-<book>`, `guide-<book>-page|topic|example-<slug>`, `guide-<book>-<slug>`. **"Try it" editors (2026-11-09, record §9):** the notes book's `markdown`, `lists`, `own` and `money` pages carry a card whose stage is the real live editor (`lib/widgets/guide_try_editor.dart`, `GuideEditorDemo` over `ModernEditorWrapper` + `ListAwarePasteController` + `EditorRenderController` + `EditorEditTracker`, six lines tall, seeded from `notesGuideSeed*`); the cards, seeds and checks are data in `lib/models/notes_guide_tries.dart` over `GuideEditorCard` / `GuideEditorStep` / `GuideEditorDocument` in `guide_content.dart` — a step is done when the document shows it (checks read the grammar modules only; fenced, commented and front-matter lines are handed to them blank), never when a control is tapped; tag, wiki, link and money taps become coach reports (`notesGuideReport*`). Ids `guide-notes-try-<slug>`, `…-caption`, `…-editor`; back/skip keep the calendar's `try-<slug>-step-back` / `try-<slug>-skip-step`. **The bar on the stage and the shortcuts page (2026-11-11, record §10):** every notes card's stage leads with the app's own `MarkdownBar` (`GuideEditorCard.bar`: built-in ids drawn in the shipped order whatever their stored visibility, the card's own shortcuts, a utility list; one unpinned row on the stage's ground, above the note so it never sits under the keyboard) and a press takes the note editor's path — focus, `MarkdownShortcutInserter` or `ShortcutApplier.resolve`, one guarded revocable write — with `{cN}` tokens counted in the demo's own memory, never in her counters; the coach lines name a button by the app's label through a placeholder, and a step is still read off the document. A fifth card on the `shortcuts` page ("Your own shortcuts") runs two real `CustomMarkdownShortcut`s (`notesGuideShortcutButtons`) and lets her make one: `GuideEditorCard.maker` puts a `markdownStart` field between the bar and the editor and her button (`ownShortcutId`) at the bar's end, and its `press` step reads `GuideEditorDocument.ownPresses`, the one step that is a tap. The wrapper reports a programmatic write through `onTextChanged` synchronously: anything a check needs is set before the write. Ids add `…-bar` and `…-maker`; a `GuideExampleEntry` may carry its own two labels (a shortcut's recipe). **The sync book and its two-phone stages (2026-11-21, record §11):** `GuideBook.sync` ("Sync and sharing", between notes and app) has ten pages in `lib/models/sync_guide_content.dart` and one "Try it" card each in `lib/models/sync_guide_tries.dart` (`GuideShareStep` / `GuideFlipStep`). The frame is `lib/widgets/guide_two_phones.dart` (`GuideTwoPhonesDemo`: the flip between "your phone" and the other one, the dot — a ring while a change travels, filled once it arrived — and the phone's bar "{phone} · {area}"; `guide_sync_phones.dart` adds the airplane toggle); the worlds are plain memory (`guide_share_world.dart`, `guide_sync_world.dart` — the engine in miniature: outbox, online flag, `deliver()`), and **a stage builds no service, bloc, DAO, setting, navigator or clipboard call and writes nothing** (D41). **Nothing is redrawn for the guide** (D43): each stage (`lib/widgets/guide_sync_try_<card>.dart`) draws the app's own rows through the public builders the real surfaces also call — `event_share_row.dart`, `share_settings_sections.dart`, `sync_settings_sections.dart`, `pairing_sheet_parts.dart` — so a change to a row there changes the stage, and a stage may leave a row out but never copy one. A step is done when the world shows it, a step about the other phone by what arrived there (D44); a card keeps ONE stage rect across every step and both phones (D55; the tallest state is laid out unseen), proved per card at 360×780 and 412×915, text scale 1.0 / 1.3 / 2.0, en/de/ro, light and dark; `test/widgets/guide_sync_heights_test.dart` prints each card's height as the page draws it (`GUIDE-HEIGHT`). The hub leads with the live "Your setup" card (`guide_setup_card.dart` over `GuideSetupReader`, facts in `references/sync-and-accounts.md`), drawn only when no search is typed; a setup row opens the screen that finishes its step and goes back to a Sync & sharing page already under the guide. **Help on the sync surfaces is a `help_outline` button in the bar** (`syncGuideHelpButton` on Sync & sharing and on the Sharing page, opening the book; `pairing-how` in the pairing sheet's header, opening the `pair` page) — never a row or section in the Sync & sharing list, whose end is pinned to the held-back and deletion sections (`sync_section_test`, `sync_held_back_section_test`). Keys `syncGuide*`; ids `guide-sync`, `guide-sync-try-<card>-…`, `guide-sync-setup`, `guide-sync-setup-<row>`, `sharing-sync-how`, `sharing-page-how`, `pairing-how`.
- **Keyboard inset**: `KeyboardInsetGuard` (mounted once by `MaterialApp.builder`, above the navigator) and `WindowInsetsResync`; read the inset through `MediaQuery` only — `View.of(context)` bypasses the guard — and never add a per-page stale-inset gate.
- **Search**: the full search runs on the device-local trigram body index (`BodySearchIndexer` + `NoteSearchDao`, v47; filled lazily, never on save — `drift-migrations/references/persistence-rules.md`), the quick search reads it too — title hits apart so the cut never drops one, `#tag` by prefix — merged with a scan of the 300 newest titles and previews under the same rules, and never starts a drain (the surface does, as it opens); `SearchBloc` keeps a pending query so a stale debounced keystroke cannot overwrite a newer one, and shows "still indexing" until the backfill ends.
- **Settings**: `SettingsService` + `SettingsKeys` (the service's areas are parts under `lib/services/settings_service/`: `store, money_and_colours, general, editor, calendar_appearance, calendar_page, overview_and_report, fasting, alerts, sharing, sync`, and `pairing_settings` for the value class); every settings page speaks the settings kit (`SettingsSectionList` / `SettingsSectionData` / `SettingsEntry` under `SettingsAppBar`), so restyling it is one app-wide decision (`docs/ui-language-adoption-roadmap.md`), never a change to one page.
- **Sync and accounts** (identity and pairing, and the per-database space sync built 2026-11-06 with the scalability round's chunked wire on top): `references/sync-and-accounts.md`; the roadmap and phase docs in `docs/cloud-sync-*.md`.
- **Sharing with the paired partner** (the calendar side, folders and the partner's notes, and alerts that stay fresh with the app closed; built 2026-11-12 → 14): `ShareService` + `ShareBloc`, `lib/pages/sharing_page.dart` behind the Pairing row; records `docs/cloud-share-surfaces-roadmap.md` (§15 as built) and `docs/cloud-shared-freshness.md` (§12 as built). Three rules for every area: **a surface asks `ShareService` before it offers an edit**, **a partner's rows never reach a report, an export or an unasked alert**, and **nothing but the app's own isolate opens the database while the app is alive** — the one run that may open it without the app follows the handshake of `lib/services/shared_refresh/`. Facts: `references/sync-and-accounts.md` — "Sharing, the surfaces", "the data path", "the folders' and notes' surfaces", "fresh while the app is closed"; the calendar's rules in the `calendar-events` skill.
- **Permissions** (notifications, alarms, the Android 13+ flow): `references/permissions.md`.
- **Theme**: explicit `AppTheme` + `SurfaceRoles` (a seed cannot reproduce the approved palette); folder icons are `primary`, the mock carries one accent — guarded by `test/constants/app_theme_test.dart`.

## Good defaults for a new tracking feature

Prefer note markdown content, a counter or a setting over a new table; a new table or column is a migration plus backup and import/export coverage plus a DAO test. Every new service or bloc ships with a focused suite against fakes. A new screen speaks one of the four idioms of the `ui-language` skill.
